# Demo script (≤100s · human voice)

**Positioning:** Clubs run ticket ops. CleanDeed is the **title policy** any venue attaches — not another club simulator.

Film with **Preview → Film ready** for the click path, then cut to **Live** explorer receipts (withdraw refused) so judges see both story and chain.

1. **0–12s** — Open: earning seats change hands mid-payout with leftover claims. CleanDeed is title insurance: Diligence → Lab → rebound.
2. **12–24s** — Club Console · **two venues** on one contract (create / switch) · Live RPC · back to **Preview · Film ready · Seat #3**.
3. **24–36s** — Seat desk · **0 keys** · pending cut · FREE · click **List @ 100** · call out **sign-epoch bump** (foreign-chain seal).
4. **36–48s** — Diligence **PROVABLY CLEAN** · cast **priya** · **Buy mid-payout** · REBOUND_TO_BUYER · tip BTC/SOL **unchanged**.
5. **48–62s** — Transfer Lab · old owner withdraw · held-sig · listed drain · **EXPECTED FAIL** + explorer hashes.
6. **62–74s** — Cut to explorer · panic `withdraw refused: not current owner` · same vault tip addresses.
7. **74–88s** — `/verify` claim matrix · `pnpm heist` (no wallet) · `@quitclaim/sdk` `assertCleanToBuy`.
8. **88–100s** — Close: “Any venue. Seller keeps nothing.” Track **`nfts` only**.
